# IDD-0021 - Scrollbar mouse interaction specification

Related:
- IDD-0020 (unified vertical scrollbar presentation)
- IDD-0014 (Quick View Recent changes scrolling)
- IDD-0062 (reusable UI components and scrolling)
- IDD-0067 (committed-frame input, mouse capture and resize)

## Goal and ownership

An interactive **vertical scrollbar** offers predictable mouse
navigation for a scrollable list or viewport. Mouse input must use
the same shared scrollbar state as keyboard, wheel and selected-item
navigation, without accidentally activating content beneath it.

IDD-0020 owns the shared scrollbar's visual placement and rendering;
this document owns arrow, track and thumb mouse semantics.
IDD-0062 owns generic reusable component behavior and IDD-0067
owns input routing, accepted-frame geometry and pointer capture.
Quick View's specialized `Recent changes` behavior is owned by
IDD-0014, even when it reuses these scrollbar mechanics.

## Mouse behavior

- A scrollbar has active mouse targets only when its viewport has
  content to scroll and it has enough height for interactive
  geometry. A hidden, disabled, clipped or non-scrollable bar
  must not expose a stale active scrollbar target.
- Left-clicking the **decrease/increase arrow** moves the
  first visible item by one item toward the corresponding edge.
  The resulting position is clamped to the valid range.
- Left-clicking the **track above/below the thumb** moves the
  viewport by one visible page in that direction. Repeated
  clicks are bounded by the start/end of the content.
- Left-pressing the **thumb** begins a drag with a captured
  pointer. Dragging adjusts the first visible item
  proportionally to track position while preserving the
  original pointer offset within the thumb. Movement outside
  the scrollbar remains routed to the captured drag.
- Releasing the captured left mouse button ends the drag.
  An invalidated target or owner, a covering modal layer or
  other capture loss also terminates it; the pointer must
  not continue scrolling a stale component.
- Scroll positions and thumb hit regions use committed
  viewport/layout state. An accepted resize can rebase a
  surviving drag against the new track geometry; a rejected
  frame never changes the active drag or mouse targets.
- Arrow or track input is handled by the scrollbar while
  the target is active, including clicks at scrolling
  boundaries. Normal item clicks outside the scrollbar and
  other unrelated pointer input retain their usual behavior.
- Shared scrollbar interaction applies in scrollable panels,
  result lists, history/completion popups, menus and forms
  that support routed mouse input. A component may add
  selection-visibility rules, but must keep the selected
  item, viewport and scrollbar state coherent.

## Non-goals

- Horizontal scrollbar behavior or independent new
  scrollbar drawing/layout rules (IDD-0020 owns rendering).
- Redefining mouse wheel behavior, Quick View's general
  navigation, or the full-screen editor/viewer scroll policies.
  Quick View `Recent changes` remains defined by IDD-0014.
- Mandatory hover, pressed animations or a separate
  per-dialog mouse capture mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Arrow clicks scroll by one item and track clicks by a
   visible page in the correct direction, clamped at
   both boundaries.
2. Thumb drag is proportional, preserves grab offset,
   keeps working outside the track under capture and
   stops on release or invalidation.
3. Interactive scrollbar hit targets are absent when
   the component cannot scroll; unrelated mouse clicks
   continue to reach their proper owners.
4. Mouse scrolling operates on the same viewport as
   the list or form content and never produces a stale
   selection or incorrect background click.
5. Resize and redraw never publish rejected mouse geometry;
   an accepted resize can rebase an active, still-valid drag.

## Verification

Exercise arrow and track clicks from intermediate positions
and at both boundaries. Drag the thumb from different grab
offsets, outside its original hit rectangle, and release
it. Cover non-scrollable and too-short viewports, disappearance
of the scrollbar, covering modal layers and cancellation of
capture. Test accepted resize during dragging and rejected
resize without changing committed drag geometry. Verify
panel/list/form routing and normal item activation outside
the scrollbar, including Quick View `Recent changes`
without passing its wheel input to the underlying panel.
